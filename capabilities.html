Full Service Offerings

UNMATCHED CAPABILITIES ACROSS INDIA & BEYOND

Trafotech Systems provides integrated turnkey solutions for extra-high-voltage substations, transmission networks, power transformer safety, and advanced grid telemetry.

01 / Substation EPC

EPC OF 400KV EHV SUBSTATIONS & TRANSMISSION LINES

We deliver complete Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) turnkey packages for extra-high-voltage AIS and GIS substations up to 400 kV and 765 kV.

•

Design, Engineering & Sizing

Earth mat calculations, lightning protection coverage (rolling sphere), structural load analysis, short circuit withstand calculations, and bay layout optimization.

•

Procurement & Material Management

Supply of high-voltage circuit breakers, disconnectors, CTs, PTs, isolators, surge arresters, tubular busbars, hardware fittings, and control panels.

•

Civil, Structural & Gantry Erection

Heavy foundations, transformer plinths, firewalls, oil pits, lattice towers, and equipment supporting structures.

02 / Critical Asset Protection

COMPLETE PROTECTION FOR POWER & GENERATOR TRANSFORMERS

Power and generating transformers are the most valuable assets in the power grid. Trafotech Systems provides end-to-end protective schemes that safeguard these massive units from internal and external electrical faults.

•

Numerical Relay Integration

Differential (87T), Restricted Earth Fault (REF), Directional Overcurrent, Earth Fault (50/51/51N), and Overfluxing (V/f) protection schemes.

•

Condition Monitoring & Diagnostics

Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA), online moisture and partial discharge monitoring, bushing power factor testing, and winding temperature monitoring.

03 / Live Line Operations

HOT / LIVE LINE MAINTENANCE FOR 400 KV SYSTEMS

Grid shutdowns cause astronomical commercial losses and power disruptions. Trafotech Systems provides specialized hot-line maintenance using insulated tools, hot-sticks, and barehand techniques on energized 400 kV systems.

•

Insulator String Replacement Under Voltage

Punctured or flashover insulator disc replacement without de-energizing the 400kV line.

•

Hardware & Conductor Repair

Vibration damper re-alignment, bundle spacer replacement, thermo-vision hot-spot detection, and clamp rectification.

04 / Explosion Prevention

TRANSFORMER FIRE SAFETY & NITROGEN INJECTION (NIFPS)

Transformer fires can escalate catastrophically. Trafotech Systems designs, installs, and tests Nitrogen Injection Fire Protection and Extinguishing Systems complying with Central Electricity Authority (CEA) safety guidelines.

•

Rapid Decompression & Oil Evacuation

Instantaneous detection of internal overpressure and opening of fast-release oil valves to prevent tank rupture.

•

Nitrogen Purging & Cooling

High-pressure nitrogen injection stirs and quenches the tank within seconds, eliminating oxygen and extinguishing any flash fire.

05 / Digital Grid Automation

INTEGRATED COMMAND & CONTROL SYSTEM (ICCS) & SCADA

Modern smart grids require seamless digital communication. We implement IEC 61850 substation automation architecture, GOOSE messaging, synchrophasor integration, and central dispatch control.

•

IEC 61850 Substation Gateway

Station bus and process bus integration connecting Intelligent Electronic Devices (IEDs) with master dispatch centers.

•

Telemetry & Remote Breaker Operation

Microsecond timestamping, sequence-of-events recording, fault disturbance analysis, and cybersecurity protocols.
